How FreelanceHub Works

A modern multi-category marketplace combining the direct ease of Fiverr with the flexibility of Upwork.

Track 1: Direct Service Purchase (Fiverr Model)

Instant Gigs with Clear Pricing

Need quick turnaround on a defined deliverable? Freelancers create service listings with 3 transparent package tiers: Basic, Standard, and Premium.

  • Select Package: View exact delivery days, included revisions, and features.
  • Escrow Checkout: Funds are paid through Razorpay test checkout and held securely by FreelanceHub.
  • Review & Release: Approve work or request revisions. Once approved, the freelancer is paid instantly minus a 10% platform fee.

Track 2: Custom Projects & Bidding (Upwork Model)

Custom Requirements & Milestone Workspaces

Have complex or multi-stage specifications? Clients post custom projects, and qualified freelancers bid with tailored price proposals.

  • Post Requirements: Set scope, deadline, fixed or hourly budget, and milestone stages.
  • Compare Proposals: Evaluate freelancer portfolios, cover letters, and delivery timelines.
  • Dedicated Workspace: Track progress, communicate via chat, and release milestones as deliverables land.
